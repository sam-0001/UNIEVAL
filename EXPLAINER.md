## 1. What is UniEval?

UniEval is **not** another generic coding bootcamp or generic LMS filled with random video tutorials. 

It is an **engineering university study and evaluation companion** designed from the ground up around the exact curriculum patterns, insem/endsem marking schemes, and examination standards of **Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU)**.

UniEval brings together everything an engineering student needs to excel academically across all four years:
- **First Year (FE)**: Foundational common engineering curriculum.
- **Second Year (SE)**: 12 branch-specific engineering foundations.
- **Third Year (TE)**: 9 branch advanced NEP specializations and electives.
- **Final Year (BE)**: Advanced electives, capstone project tools, and viva voce defense preparation.

## 2. The Problems UniEval Solves

Every semester, over 150,000 engineering students across Pune, Nashik, and Ahmednagar face the same chronic academic hurdles:

### 1. The "Scattered Study Material" Trap
Syllabus copies change (e.g., transition to the **SPPU 2024 Credit Pattern / NEP 2020**), but verified reference materials remain scattered across unorganized WhatsApp groups, expired Telegram links, and illegible photocopy (Xerox) shop printouts riddled with missing diagrams and outdated sections.

### 2. Ambiguous Insem vs. Endsem Exam Focus
SPPU splits semester assessments into:
- **In-Semester Exam (Insem — 30 Marks)**: Covering Units 1 & 2.
- **End-Semester Exam (Endsem — 70 Marks)**: Covering Units 3, 4, 5 & 6.  
Students often waste hundreds of hours studying out-of-scope chapters because traditional textbooks don't clearly map topics to Insem and Endsem requirements.

### 3. Lack of Verified Exam Intelligence & Marking Schemes
University exam evaluators look for specific point-by-point derivations, standard circuit schematics, and university marking scheme formats. Generic internet tutorials fail to teach how answers must be structured on physical SPPU exam answer sheets.

### 4. High KT (Backlog) Rates in Critical Subjects
Difficult subjects like *Engineering Mathematics (M1, M2, M3)*, *Engineering Mechanics*, *Data Structures & Algorithms*, *Signals & Systems*, and *Fluid Dynamics* cause immense stress and backlog fees when students lack structured, step-by-step guidance.

## 6. Technical & Security Pillars

- **Digital Rights Management (DRM)**: Documents encrypted with AES-256-GCM at rest; video chunks encrypted with AES-128. Raw storage bucket URLs are completely masked from network inspection.
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Installable directly onto Android, iOS, Windows, and Mac for native, one-tap mobile home screen access.
- **Dynamic Viewport Standard (`100dvh`)**: Mobile screens are stabilized against iOS Safari and Android Chrome address bar jitter.
- **Cloudflare R2 Zero-Egress Storage**: Ensures multi-gigabyte video playlists and PDF notes stream with zero egress fee penalties, keeping student pricing low and sustainable.
- **High-Concurrency Scalability**: Singleflight request deduplication and an in-memory decryption cache allow 10,000+ concurrent students to study smoothly the night before exams.
